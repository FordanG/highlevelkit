# Smartlead

Unlimited cold email under $100/month

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/smartlead
Provider: https://smartlead.ai
Categories: Communication, Workflow Automation

Smartlead offers unlimited email sending with volume-based pricing that's perfect for agencies scaling their outreach. Unlike per-profile pricing tools, Smartlead lets you use unlimited profiles and send unlimited emails. Ideal for running cold email campaigns parallel to your GHL workflows.

## Listed features
- Unlimited email profiles
- Volume-based pricing model
- Advanced warmup system
- Email rotation and deliverability
- Campaign analytics dashboard
- Inbox rotation for better delivery

## Use cases
- Cold Email Outreach
- Email Marketing
- Lead Generation
