# Apollo.io

B2B contact database with millions of verified contacts

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/apollo-io
Provider: https://apollo.io
Categories: Data Enrichment, CRM Tools

Apollo.io is a sales intelligence and engagement platform with millions of verified B2B contacts and companies. Find leads with advanced filtering by industry, job title, location, and company size. Import quality leads directly into GoHighLevel to fuel your campaigns.

## Listed features
- 10,000 email addresses/month (99/mo plan)
- Millions of verified contacts and companies
- Advanced filtering (industry, job title, location)
- Company size and technographic data
- Sales engagement tools
- CSV export to GHL

## Use cases
- Lead Generation
- Data Enrichment
- Sales Intelligence
