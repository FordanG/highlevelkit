# Vidyard

Professional video messaging for sales

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/vidyard
Provider: https://www.vidyard.com
Categories: Video, Communication, Analytics & Reporting

Vidyard is a professional screen recorder and video hosting platform for sales teams. Create high-quality, impactful videos that effectively articulate your message. Embed videos in your GHL email campaigns to increase engagement and reply rates. Track who watches your videos and for how long.

## Listed features
- Screen and webcam recording
- Video hosting and sharing
- Viewer analytics and tracking
- Thumbnail customization
- Video CTAs and forms
- Chrome extension for quick recording

## Use cases
- Video Prospecting
- Client Communication
- Analytics & Reporting
