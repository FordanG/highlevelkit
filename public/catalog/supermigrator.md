# Super Migrator

Seamless GoHighLevel account migration

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/supermigrator
Provider: https://supermigrator.com
Categories: Workflow Automation, Integration Platforms

Super Migrator is a specialized service for migrating GoHighLevel accounts. Easily transfer your entire GHL setup including contacts, workflows, funnels, and settings between accounts or agencies. Save hours of manual work with automated migration tools.

## Listed features
- Account migration service
- Contact and data transfer
- Workflow migration
- Funnel and page cloning
- Settings preservation
- Agency-to-agency transfers
