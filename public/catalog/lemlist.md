# Lemlist

Personalized outreach at scale

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/lemlist
Provider: https://lemlist.com
Categories: Communication, Workflow Automation

Lemlist connects to GoHighLevel workflows to sync contacts and start personalized, multi-channel outreach sequences. Automatically trigger actions based on prospect activity and engagement.

## Listed features
- Personalized email sequences
- Multi-channel outreach
- Automated follow-ups
- Email warm-up
- A/B testing
- LinkedIn integration

## Use cases
- Lead Generation
- Email Marketing
- Client Communication
