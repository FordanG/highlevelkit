AI-powered personalized video at scale

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/potion
Provider: https://www.sendpotion.com

Create multiple personalized videos featuring your own face and voice with automatically generated subtitles, logos, images, and GIFs. - AI-generated personalized videos
- Your face and voice in every video

- Custom landing pages with domains
- Calendar embed integration
- Bulk video generation
