# Snitcher

Uncover your hidden website visitors

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/snitcher
Provider: https://snitcher.com
Categories: Data Enrichment, Analytics & Reporting

Snitcher is a B2B visitor identification platform that reveals companies visiting your website in real-time. Identify, enrich, and engage with anonymous website visitors automatically. Score and prioritize accounts showing buying intent, reveal verified details of key decision-makers, and trigger automations across your entire GTM stack.

## Listed features
- Real-time visitor identification
- Company and contact reveal
- Lead scoring and prioritization
- GA4 B2B enrichment
- Workflow automation triggers
- API access for integrations

## Use cases
- Lead Generation
- Data Enrichment
- Sales Intelligence
