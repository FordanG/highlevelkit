# Hippo Video

Video prospecting for every sales stage

Canonical page: https://highlevelkit.com/apps/hippo-video
Provider: https://www.hippovideo.io
Categories: Video, Communication

Hippo Video stands out with unique features and sales video flows that cater to various stages of your sales process. Create personalized video messages, screen recordings, and video emails that can be embedded in your GHL campaigns. Share via email, LinkedIn, SMS, or social media.

## Listed features
- Sales video flows for each stage
- Screen + webcam recording
- Video email integration
- Personalized video thumbnails
- Video analytics dashboard
- CRM integrations

## Use cases
- Video Prospecting
- Client Communication
- Lead Generation
